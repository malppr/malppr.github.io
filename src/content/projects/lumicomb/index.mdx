## Overview

LumiComb was created in 2021, set in a post-COVID recovery context. The aim was to encourage social interaction through a walkable light exhibition with dynamic, interactive light patterns.

## How it works

The exhibit is a field of hexagonal tiles that detect when someone stands on them. Each tile changes colour, and together they form an overall pattern. Several patterns respond to people: linking visitors with illuminated paths, or sending out expanding rings as people move between tiles.

We built a small-scale prototype from 3D-printed wood filament, motion sensors and LED strips to test the interaction patterns, and two full-scale units from recycled wooden planks with LED lighting and motion sensors.
