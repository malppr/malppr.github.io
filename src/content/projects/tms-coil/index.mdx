## Overview

Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) is a non-invasive treatment that stimulates the brain to treat conditions such as depression.

This project developed a **static negative-permeability metasurface** coil that projects virtual figure-8 loops below the surface of the head, giving a more focused point of stimulation. We hand-wound prototype coils and tested them with a high-voltage setup and electric-field detectors, including through a pig's head, to validate the design.

The research contributed to a PhD thesis but, due to unforeseen delays, was not published.
