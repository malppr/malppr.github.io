## Overview

This project began at a design workshop for a children's library, where we came up with the idea of an interactive robotic bookshelf that engages children and encourages them to read. It grew into a formal study: we built a prototype bookshelf and tested how it affected children's reading behaviour, comparing it against the pull of an iPad.

The bookshelf is shaped like an elephant. Its trunk is a **continuum mechanism**, a flexible, cable-driven robot that bends smoothly like a real trunk, and the whole robot can be teleoperated to interact with children.

## What I did

- Designed and built **all of the robot's hardware and software** single-handedly: the frame, the continuum trunk, electronics and remote-control system.
- Planned and wrote the initial **study proposal**, which was approved by the university's Institutional Review Board (IRB).
- The study was published at **IEEE RO-MAN 2023**.
