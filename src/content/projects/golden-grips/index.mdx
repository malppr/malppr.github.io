## Overview

A phone adapter that helps elderly users hold and use their phones more easily. It has an ergonomic grip and an attachable baseplate with loops for an adjustable strap, so the phone can be secured to the hand.

The brief was a functional souvenir for the school, so scalability and cost mattered. The design uses accessible materials, mainly 3D-printed parts and an inexpensive strap.

## Process

3D printing drove a fast, iterative design process: we tested and refined the grip and baseplate over several iterations for comfort. We also evaluated scalable manufacturing methods so the design could be mass-produced efficiently.
