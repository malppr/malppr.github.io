## Overview

At the Deep Cognition and Language Research (DeCLaRe) Lab, I worked on a text-to-speech model for **zero-shot** scenarios: given just a 3-second recording of a new speaker, it generates high-quality speech in that speaker's voice.

## How it works

The architecture uses a large language model (FLAN-T5) that takes words and phonemes as input, and produces speech through **reverse diffusion**: starting from noise and iteratively refining it into a speech representation, conditioned on the text and the short voice prompt.

## What I did

- Co-designed the model and led its implementation.
- Built the full training and evaluation pipeline in **PyTorch** and **DeepSpeed**, training on 8× A100 GPUs.
- Explored alternative architectures, including GANs, VAEs and Mixture-of-Experts models.
