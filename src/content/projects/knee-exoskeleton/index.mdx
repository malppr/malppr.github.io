## Overview

This knee exoskeleton uses **pneumatic actuators** to assist with walking. The actuators are pouches of a novel design that contract when inflated, powered by small air compressors carried in a waist bag.

## How it works

An Arduino controls a set of air valves that direct the airflow. A flex sensor at the knee detects the wearer's movement and triggers the actuators to provide assistance at the right moment.

For comfort and support, the knee straps are made from stretchable fabric using fabric printing, with a 3D-printed brace for structure.

## What I did

I led the team, designed the braces and the pneumatic pouches, and oversaw the integration of all the components.
